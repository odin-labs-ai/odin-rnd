TRACK A

Compute & token sponsors.

For model providers, brokers and compute platforms that want their models measured on real work instead of a public benchmark everyone has already tuned for.

What you get
  • Real workloads: your tokens or credits run inside the factory, on the reviews, fixes and research it does every day.
  • Honest measurement, under the same pre-registered rules as every other provider, with the method and data published.
  • A written case study when an experiment includes you, and a conversation about routing real traffic, agreed in writing if it happens.
What we ask
  • Token credits or compute for the experiments and work that include you.
  • Research or volume pricing we can use for Odin's own routing.
  • Consent where your terms ask for it before we name you next to a number.
How it starts
A call about what you want to learn, then a short proposal: which workloads, which measures, which spend bound. Nothing runs until it is agreed in writing.

Our independence policy

  • Sponsors never buy results. The question, the measure, the decision rule and the spend bound are published before any counted call. A result that goes against a sponsor stays published.
  • No benchmark without leave. We do not benchmark a provider whose terms do not allow it, and we name a provider next to a measured number only when its published terms allow it, as recorded in data/provider-terms.json. Until then a provider is shown by letter.
  • Same rules for everyone. Sponsored or not, every provider is measured the same way. Sponsorship changes what we can afford to run, never how it is judged.

More for providers: what a provider gets and what we ask, and the brief, to print or forward.

TRACK B

Company-AGI partners.

For companies that want their own governed factory, following the Odin vision: agents that do real work, a record of every decision, and humans who approve what matters. A long-term partnership, not a project.

What you get
  • Early access to lab results and to new factory capabilities before they are published.
  • Your own factory, set up and measured with us, on your workflows and your rules.
  • A say in the roadmap: new benchmarks and acceptance checks designed around your work.
What we ask
  • A long-term commitment and a shared view of where this is going.
  • Willingness to make your own work measurable, by the definition below.
  • Something structural: real cases to work on, a network, or compute, not only budget for a project.
How it starts
An exploratory conversation, then an evaluation or sprint on a real workflow of yours. The partnership grows from what that shows.
LEVEL

Exploratory

A conversation and a look at the work. Early sight of public lab reports before wider publication.

LEVEL

Active

A completed evaluation or sprint and an agreement on the next. Throughput reporting for your own workflow.

LEVEL

Strategic

Recurring work, plus your own cases or network brought in. Co-design of a benchmark or acceptance check.

What "company AGI" means here, measurably

We use the term only for things we can count. Where we cannot count it yet, we say so.

Throughput
Merged changes per day that the factory delivers. Instrumented internally; not yet published as a pre-registered record.
Autonomy
The share of merged work authored by an agent and approved by a human, with no human author. Not yet measured.

Throughput says how much work the factory delivers, not how independently. We will not present throughput as autonomy, and a partner sees only figures that are measured.

TRACK C

Innovation sprints & research licences.

For teams with a hard question: a task they do not trust agents with yet, a risky migration, a security weakness, or a frontier direction. Any tool or vendor fits, open source or commercial.

What you get
  • A fixed-scope sprint with a fixed end, and evidence at the end of every phase: a working trial, a measured result, or a clear "this does not work".
  • A licence to the results: reports, datasets, methods and tuned acceptance gates, for use inside your organisation.
  • A decision after each phase, together with us: continue, adjust or stop.
What we ask
  • A named reviewer on your side and access to the workflow in question.
  • Agreement up front on scope, acceptance criteria and what may be published.
  • Acceptance that a negative result is a valid result.
How it starts
A conversation about the problem, then a written sprint charter: the task, the examples, the measure and the timebox.

How research licences work

  • The intellectual property stays with Odin Labs Holding B.V. The lab licenses the results; it does not transfer the method, the sealed test sets or general improvements that come out of the work.
  • What can be licensed: reports, datasets, methods (how to pre-register an evaluation, how to build an acceptance check) and gates tuned to your codebase or workflow.
  • Any tool or vendor. The work can use open-source or commercial tools alike. Open source is not a condition of working with us.

Funding the research.

The lab's research and development qualifies for innovation subsidies. An MIT application for Odin Next has been submitted; the decision is pending. No grant has been awarded, and this page claims none.

What this page does not do.

It names no prices and no current or future partners, and it describes no signed partnership: none is signed yet. A partner is listed on the providers page only after an agreement is signed and on record.

Every figure the lab has measured is on what we have measured, and not, each linked to its record.
