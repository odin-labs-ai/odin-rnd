A brief for
model providers.

Odin routes its own work across many models and brokers. The Odin R&D lab measures what that costs and how providers behave, and publishes each result with its method and data, whatever it shows. This page is meant to be read in a couple of minutes, printed or forwarded. The longer version is the page for model providers.

What the lab offers.

  • A place in a public, pre-registered benchmark where your models or routing fit the question, under the same rules as every other provider. The question, the measure, the decision rule and the spend bound are published before any counted call.
  • A written case study: a field note with the method, the result and its data, on this site.
  • A conversation about Odin's own routing. Any volume would be agreed in writing; none is promised here.

What the lab asks.

  • Research credits for the experiments that include you. Credits never buy a result.
  • Research or volume pricing, stated in writing.
  • Consent where your terms ask for it. We name a provider next to a measured number only when its published terms allow it; until then it is shown by letter.

The rule for this page.

No projection, forecast, logo or partner appears here. Each number is read from the experiment record it links to when the site is built, and the build stops if a number is not read from an experiment record or no longer matches it (scripts/number-provenance.mjs). What we have measured, and not and how we run experiments hold the rest.
