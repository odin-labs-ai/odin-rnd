CI Witness

The target is fixed.
The correction still fails.

An agent can make the failing assertion pass while breaking something else—or by removing the check. CI Witness deliberately tries both and requires the acceptance check to reject them.

A fix must retain the evidence that could reject it.

The runner requires the exact target assertion, the case-specific boundary and both regression assertions. Suppressed checks cannot qualify. Unknown exceptions stay unresolved. Timezone, ordering and configuration cases apply the same discipline under explicit conditions.

These examples test the acceptance mechanism. They do not measure an agent’s performance on unseen work.

Inspect the recording.

Recorded fixture run; the execution environment is identified below. These Java examples, candidate tests and corrections were authored for this demonstration. Selecting a case reads saved evidence; it does not execute code or an agent.

Acceptance concerns the stated evidence check. An expected rejection can demonstrate that the check works; it does not mean the tested behavior passed. Fixture results are not customer savings, held-out performance or a guarantee of broader correctness.

Reproduce on your machine.

From the repository root, with Node 22 and JDK 21 installed. Runs trusted bundled fixtures locally.

node demos/ci-witness/run.mjs --out /tmp/ci-witness.json

Bring one decision to the bench.

A proposed pilot for a team deciding whether this evidence would improve its engineering review.

Proposed delivery cap: 12 engineering hours for this bounded scope, subject to input review and agreement. A planning hypothesis, not a service promise or measured saving.

Prepare a pilot briefDownload CI Witness proposal
Good fit
One known environment-sensitive Java failure in a trusted nonproduction test suite.
You bring
A runnable fixture; approved environment configurations; target assertion and expected boundary and regression outcomes.
You take away
A controlled matrix, baseline/reproducer/correction transcripts and an acceptance receipt with unresolved cases disclosed.
Acceptance to agree
The same target failure reproduces; the correction passes its target, boundary and regression checks without suppressing them.
Outside this pilot
General autonomous diagnosis or repair, production changes, flaky-rate estimates and arbitrary untrusted code.

Measure whether it earns its place.

Agree a comparable baseline, acceptance criteria and a stop condition before work starts. Record setup, execution, human review and rework time, plus tool costs and the full cost of labor. Compare total effort and accepted outcomes; disclose unresolved work.

The demo establishes a mechanism to inspect. Whether it reduces your team's cost or risk is a pilot question. Agree scope, fees, access, data handling and delivery terms before sharing code or data. The brief is a manual preparation step; nothing is sent automatically.

What would convince your team that an agent’s fix is safe to accept?

Bring one reproducible failure and the checks your team cannot afford to lose. We can define the reproduction and acceptance conditions, compare candidate corrections, and record investigation and review effort alongside failures.

The proposed work is the acceptance system around your workflow: an explicit contract, checks that can reject a plausible but wrong result, and a record another engineer can reproduce. The small Java example above makes one part of that approach inspectable.
