Factory intelligence and telemetry: the horizon

HORIZON · STATED 01 OCT 2026 · RUNG H0 IN PROGRESS

Where the software factory's own records could take it: a ladder from instrumentation today to a factory whose routing, scoping and reviewing are learned from its own priced and graded outcomes. Each rung has a milestone and a kill criterion, stated before the work, and the page shows the measured numbers that decide where we stand on the first rung.

The dot

H3, 2029–30. A factory that learns from its own outcomes. Routing, scoping, reviewing and the choice of context form are decided by models trained on the factory's joined, priced and graded traces. Cache handoff between models (EXP 008) is one possible organ of that system, not the whole of it.

A goal for every later rung, not yet built: every handoff that is not plain text also emits a human-readable receipt, a digest and a hash of what was handed over.

Every rung's dates, thresholds and kill criteria were set in Odin Labs' private planning record, committed before either H0 measurement was taken; this page restates them in neutral words. Since then two rungs lost an item each, and nothing was added or moved.

How big the prize is today

How much a cache handoff could save today, measured before anyone builds one. Today the factory has no open-weight to open-weight handoff, so latent handoff is a lab result the factory could adopt, not a current saving.

  • Subagent handoffs from an open-weight model to an open-weight model: 0.00% (0 of 2,077). Every subagent receiver is a closed-weight model, so the zero is structural: there is nothing for a cache transfer to apply to today.
  • First-turn context, median: 270,485 tokens (n = 2077). p90 is 284,166 tokens; the distribution is bimodal (p25 is 78,897).
  • First turns larger than the D1 receiver's whole context window (131,072 tokens): 66.20% (1,375 of 2,077). A small open receiver could not hold most real first turns at all.
  • D1 receiver prefill of 16,384 tokens on the Mac, median: 7,673.4 ms (n = 5). 5 timed repetitions after a warm-up, on a shared machine (1-minute load average 57.86 right after the curve): an order of magnitude, not a benchmark.
  • First-turn tokens freshly prefilled rather than served from the provider's prompt cache: 89.10% (387,251,810 of 434,609,344). An upper bound on what a handoff could remove, not the saving: it includes each receiver's own fixed system prompt and tool definitions, which a handoff would not replace.

The ladder

H0 · Oct–Nov 2026: Instrument the factory and run one clean experiment.

Milestones.

  • A flight-recorder event schema is frozen, so every agent step, handoff, verdict, cost and outcome can be written in one shape. Now: met (source).
  • At least 80% of the last 30 days of review verdicts join to the outcome of their pull request. Now: 91.56%, met (measured).
  • At least 50% of routing rows carry a recorded cost rather than an unknown one. Now: 0.88%, not yet met (measured).
  • EXP 008 is pre-registered, run, independently reviewed and published on public baselines, whatever its result. Now: in progress (source).
  • A prize-size report: how large first-turn contexts are, multiplied by the measured local prefill curve. It says how much a cache handoff could save before anyone builds one. Now: met on publication of this page (source).
  • Open telemetry dataset v0: flight-recorder rows from already-public sources only, de-identified, with the tenant floor applied and failing closed. Rows derived from private telemetry wait for H2. Now: met on publication of this page (source).

Kill criteria.

  • Outcome-join coverage below 50%: stop the learning work and fix the instrumentation first. Now: not triggered (measured).
  • The locked model pair misses the text re-prefill accuracy floor (A0 ≥ 0.75 on the long contexts): publish that small receivers cannot do this gate, and add no further transfer arm.
  • Neither candidate pair can be run on the hardware: report EXP 008 as uninformative (a hardware failure, not a finding about the receivers), and add no further transfer arm.

H1 · Q4 2026–Q1 2027: First predictions from the records.

Milestones.

  • A time-split "will it land?" baseline reaches AUROC ≥ 0.70 on pull requests it never saw. Not yet mature: evaluable from 27 OCT 2026. Outcomes use the plan's 14-day horizon, and a record counts only once it is 28 days old. The first 30-day time-split window closes on this date.
  • Calibration (Brier) curves are published for at least three review lanes. Not yet mature: evaluable from 08 OCT 2026. Outcomes use the plan's 14-day horizon, and a record counts only once it is 28 days old. The first records mature on this date; at least 30 per lane in at least three lanes is expected around late October 2026.
  • A feasibility note on handing state between hybrid models, whose layers mix attention with recurrent or feed-forward-only blocks.

Kill criteria.

  • AUROC below 0.62 on two monthly windows: drop the outcome model. Not yet mature: evaluable from 26 NOV 2026. Outcomes use the plan's 14-day horizon, and a record counts only once it is 28 days old. The kill needs two monthly windows; the second closes around this date.

H2 · 2027: Learned decisions, tested against the rules they would replace.

Milestones.

  • A learned router, A/B-tested on internal lanes that serve no paying customer, shows at least 20% lower cost per landed outcome than the current routing rubric.
  • The first specialist model post-trained on factory traces ships.
  • Public dataset v1.
  • A cache-handoff bus on one local lane, only if a handoff shows at least 2× end to end and a KV-compatible model pair is actually deployed.

Kill criteria.

  • The router beats the rubric by less than 10%: keep the rubric.
  • Cache handoff below 1.3× end to end: park the bus.

H3 · 2029–30: The dot.

Milestones.

  • Cost per landed outcome and the calibration of P(lands) are published every month from committed records.

Kill criteria.

  • If specialists and the bus never beat text handoff with frontier models, narrow the goal to telemetry-driven routing.

What this does not establish

  • The H0 measurements and the prize-size figures come from a private repository and cannot be re-run from this site; each source line names the committed record and its sha256, so a later public release can be checked against them. Dataset v0 is the exception: it is built only from this site's own published experiment records and is published here.
  • Only aggregates of private telemetry are published. No private row, tenant, customer, prompt or log is published; rows derived from private telemetry wait for H2.
  • The thresholds were set before the measurements (see above), on the same day. The cost milestone is judged on routing rows, as set; the five-source share is shown only as context and is never the milestone value. A change to any of them is published here as a dated revision, never as a silent edit.
  • No KV-compatible sender-receiver model pair is deployed on the factory floor today; the local decoders are hybrids. Any cache-handoff rung waits on that.
