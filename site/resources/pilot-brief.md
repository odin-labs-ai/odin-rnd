# Proposed pilot brief

Blank template for discussion. No pilot, commercial price or outcome is agreed by completing this brief. Do not include confidential inputs or personal data. Review before sharing through a separate contact channel.

## One workflow
To agree: describe one bounded workflow, its users and its start/end points.

## Intended outcome
To agree: what useful change would justify the work?

## Current baseline
To agree: how is the work done today, and what can be measured reliably?

## Samples and permission
To agree: available examples, the right to use them, handling restrictions and who can approve access. Agree permissions and the reviewer/evaluation contract before sharing inputs.

## Constraints and review needs
To agree: human reviewer, exclusions, safety constraints, integrations and handover owner.

## Before starting
Agree a pilot charter, a fixed work/time budget, baseline and held-out samples under comparable conditions, thresholds and exclusions. Include all human review, rework, model and infrastructure costs. A negative or inconclusive result is valid. Decide whether to continue, change or stop from the evidence.
