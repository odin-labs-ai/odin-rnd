Odin sends model calls through several brokers and to many models. Whether that is smart, and whether it saves money, are questions we answer with experiments rather than with a sales line. This page collects what the series has measured so far. Every number on it is read from a published experiment record when the site is built, and links to that record. Questions without a published result are listed as such.

How to read this page.

Each experiment's question, measure and decision rule were frozen and published before any counted call; the methods page explains how. A broker is named next to a number only when its published terms allow it, as recorded in data/provider-terms.json; otherwise it is shown by letter.
