EXP 015: Does Odin's cost estimate match what the brokers bill?

RESEARCH NOTE / 013 · EXP 015 AMENDMENT 01 · MEASURED 28 OCT – 3 NOV 2026

Odin estimates the cost of every model call from a price book, before the broker's bill arrives. Before any savings figure can be published, that estimate has to be checked against what the brokers actually charge. EXP 015 sent one identical tiny completion through each of three brokers every 3 hours for 7 whole UTC days, and compared the price-book estimate with the cost each broker reported, call by call.

Measured over 336 counted calls across 7 days: the estimate held for two of the three brokers, and was off by a stable 10% factor for the third. None of the three brokers' terms yet clears naming it next to a measured figure (see Evidence and its boundary), so this note and its published data call them Broker X, Broker Y and Broker Z throughout, by a private assignment that is not published. Every one of the 336 attempted calls succeeded; none were imputed.

What was fixed in advance

The question, the three brokers, the two models, the capture cadence, the per-broker and fleet decision rules were published before the window opened and frozen by sha256; the record was merged 23 October 2026, and the counted window ran 2026-10-28T00:00Z to 2026-11-04T00:00Z, with billing read no earlier than 2026-11-05T00:00Z, per the pre-registration's own settlement margin for one broker's per-call figures.

  • Models: two models, each under each broker's own id, one call per model per broker every 3 hours (56 runs, 6 calls each).
  • Metric: the relative gap r = brokerReportedCost ÷ priceBookEstimate − 1, for every counted call (inside the window, HTTP 200, both a broker cost and a positive estimate).
  • Decision rule, per broker: "estimate holds" if the median |r| is at most 5% and its 95% interval sits inside ±5%; "off by a stable factor" if not holding but the interval is at most 2 points wide; otherwise "unreliable". Fewer than 50 counted calls reports as "not enough calls".
  • Decision rule, fleet: do Odin's own logged estimates cover what one broker billed the account over the same window — a ratio between 0.9 and 1.1 with at most 10% zero-estimate rows "supports a savings claim"; otherwise it does not.

Every counted call carried a broker-reported usage.cost (in-band share 100% for all three), so there is nothing imputed and nothing missing. The gap was exactly the same on every one of the 7 counted days for every broker: 10% for Broker X, 0% for Broker Y and Broker Z, which is why each 95% interval collapses to a single point. Broker X's 10% markup matches a stable factor seen in a private calibration run before this window; this note does not say more about why, since doing so would narrow which broker X is.

What the fleet half could not answer

The pre-registration's second question — whether Odin's own logged estimates, summed over the window, cover what one broker actually billed the account — needs fleet.json: counts and a ratio computed privately from Odin's routing log and that broker's account activity, by a script that runs against production billing data this lane does not hold. No fleet.json was available to this record, so the frozen analysis reports it as it is designed to: not measurable, rather than guessed or left silently absent. Whoever can run that reconciliation next should treat this as open, not as "supports" by default.

What it means

Measured: for these two models, on list-priced, uncached, tiny calls, Odin's price-book estimate is a usable stand-in for Broker Y and Broker Z's bill with no correction. For Broker X it needs a known, stable correction (multiply by 1.10) before a savings claim built on estimates would be honest. Not measured: discounts, cached calls, long prompts, reasoning tokens, or whether the fleet's logged estimates as a whole track what was actually billed.

Evidence and its boundary

Every counted call is published: the results, recomputed in CI by a test that runs the frozen analysis unchanged on the published rows themselves. Those rows carry per-call USD figures (what each broker reported, and the price-book's estimate); this note and results.json state ratios only — no absolute dollar figure is computed or published in this prose, by design, before the first counted call ran. The capture code that calls the brokers is private; its files are frozen by the sha256 the pre-registration states, which a reader can check without running it.

This record is amended: a dated amendment records the decision to publish by letter rather than by name, the same shape and the same reasoning EXP 016's own amendment recorded on 2026-10-23 for the same two brokers' terms. The pre-registration itself, frozen and merged before this amendment existed, names all three brokers in full; this amendment cannot and does not reach back and anonymise that already-public, unchangeable text. It only governs how this note, this page's data and the published rows themselves are written, from here forward.

Pre-registered experiment, amended before publication. One laptop reads and analyses published rows; nothing ran locally. Field note written 28 Nov 2026, after the window's artifacts were pulled from the 90-day-retained capture runs.
