The rule for this page.

No projection, market size or forecast appears here, and no partner is named. Each number is read from the record it links to when the site is built, and the build stops if a number is not read from an experiment record or no longer matches it (scripts/number-provenance.mjs). A sentence that compares results, such as intervals overlapping, is checked against the record the same way. These checks establish consistency with a record, not the truth of its observations, independent replication or applicability beyond its stated sample.
