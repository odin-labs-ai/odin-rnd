# Odin identity alignment — 7 September 2026

The owner requested an actual design alignment with https://odin-labs-ai.github.io/open-docs/#learn and https://odin-labs.ai/, retaining the factory feel and using Odin’s own logo.

## Fresh reference observations

Both pages were read in Chromium at 1440px. Open Docs loads Manrope for reading and Space Grotesk for headings, with body #f3f6ef, ink #18382d and header #102b22. The main website currently renders system sans on #fbf7f5, with emerald actions and the OdinLabs lockup. Do not claim the marketing runtime loads Manrope.

The Open Docs public brand asset uses the exact path and viewBox from the marketing header. Its original gradient is resolved to #39ff14 / #00ffff. It is copied unchanged into the local site and favicon. The shape is not reinterpreted. Hash and source are in asset-provenance.json; Odin retains its trademark rights.

## Design decision

Keep the factory bay, machinery and blueprint panels dark. Give the surrounding catalogue and reading areas the pale Open Docs surface, forest ink and Manrope typography. Keep Space Grotesk headings and JetBrains Mono machine records. Use the real logo consistently in the navigation, footer, machine plate and error page; link the footer to Odin Labs and Open Docs.

This preserves the distinctive factory illustration while correcting the generic placeholder identity and making the public sites recognizably related. Approved commercial positioning, experiment claims and behavior remain unchanged. Site revision becomes 0.2.0.
