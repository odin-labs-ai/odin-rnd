# One-workflow pilot delivery runbook

Use after reviewing the [readiness gates](company-readiness.md). This is a proposed procedure, not evidence of an executed pilot. Keep completed customer records and raw inputs in an approved private workspace. Share only permitted artifacts.

## From intake to a decision

1. **Qualify.** Review the [brief](../site/resources/pilot-brief.md): one workflow, current baseline, useful decision, sample permission, accountable customer reviewer and feasible scope. Decline or reshape work without a measurable boundary. Assign the delivery/commercial owners and backup; clear acceptance gates before committing.
2. **Charter.** Complete the [charter](../site/resources/pilot-charter.md) with exclusions, fixed time/cost ceiling, price, deliverables, acceptance, review rounds and stop conditions. Clear data/environment gates before receiving customer data or running work. Any scope change needs a recorded impact on budget, samples, schedule and thresholds, approved by the agreed authorities before work resumes; never silently absorb additional workflows.
3. **Freeze the comparison.** Specify baseline and candidate versions, representative development samples and separately held-out evaluation samples. Freeze sample references, scoring, quality thresholds, critical failure criteria and negative controls before evaluation. Keep held-out inputs from tuning; if exposed, record contamination and agree a fresh set. Use comparable conditions and retain deviations.
4. **Execute and account.** Run within approved access and environment boundaries. Log every attempt, failed run, intervention, review and rework against the ceiling. Exercise deliberate failure cases; preserve failures as evidence. Pause at critical failures, permission changes or budget exhaustion. The named human reviewer scores both approaches, records disagreement and approves any bounded rework before another attempt.
5. **Report honestly.** Complete the [evaluation report](../site/resources/evaluation-report.md) with baseline comparison, acceptance decisions, negative controls, uncertainty, exclusions and actual total costs. Mark missing observations unknown. Negative and inconclusive results are valid deliverables; they do not become success through selective retries. Record any post-freeze changes separately.
6. **Hand over and decide.** Have the customer owner read back operating limits, review needs and next steps, then reproduce the agreed handover instruction or export check. Record acceptance or discrepancies, artifact/access ownership, revocation, retention deadlines and who verifies deletion—including provider-held copies where applicable. Record **go / change / stop**, authority and rationale. “Go” applies only to the agreed next scope; recurrence needs its own readiness evidence.

## Blank effort and cost record

Set currency, rate basis and accounting boundaries before work. Value unpaid founder time at an agreed labor rate. Include preparation, delivery, failed attempts, review, rework and handover. Reconcile invoice/usage records with the log; do not treat missing costs as zero.

Compare contribution and repeat delivery hours with the pre-agreed threshold. Delivery contribution is not company profit: company overhead, acquisition, tax and other non-delivery costs remain outside this calculation unless explicitly allocated and disclosed.
