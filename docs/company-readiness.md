# Company readiness: evidence before commitments

This is a public working plan, not a declaration of delivery readiness. The proposed [Agent Acceptance Bench](../site/work-with-us/index.html) evaluates one engineering workflow. A completed website does not establish available capacity, customer outcomes or a deployable service. Keep customer records, assignments and commercial evidence in an agreed private workspace; never complete them in this public repository.

## Reuse what exists

| Existing asset | Useful now | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Static site, build and checks | Explain the offer and inspect public evidence; local implementation verified | No hosted agent execution or customer account service |
| Recorded BCE mechanism tests | Demonstrate explicit constraints against clean and deliberately failing fixtures | Author-controlled examples, not customer efficacy or reliability evidence |
| Browser brief and [blank brief](../site/resources/pilot-brief.md) | Prepare a description locally for deliberate sharing | No intake backend, booking or agreed engagement |
| [Charter](../site/resources/pilot-charter.md) and [report](../site/resources/evaluation-report.md) | Agree an evaluation and record its result | Blank templates, not completed deliveries |
| Private platform capabilities | Candidate reuse, subject to deployment-specific verification | Runtime suitability is unverified here; this site must not rebuild core platform logic |

Use the [delivery runbook](pilot-delivery-runbook.md) to turn these assets into a bounded engagement. Every gate below starts **unassigned / unproven**. The owner role must become a named accountable person in the private engagement record, with linked evidence and a dated decision. A role label alone cannot clear a gate.

## Prioritized gates

| When / gate | Owner role — currently unassigned | Required evidence | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before accepting: ownership and capacity | Delivery lead; commercial lead; delivery backup | Named people accepting responsibility, reviewer availability, scheduled delivery/rework hours and backup coverage within the scoped budget | Missing accountable person, backup or feasible capacity |
| Before accepting: agreement | Commercial lead with appropriate legal/data review | Authorized entities/signers; scope, access responsibilities, confidentiality, IP/licenses, price/payment, acceptance and revision/change terms; data roles and applicable subprocessors identified | Material terms or processing responsibility unresolved; no implied agreement from a brief |
| Before customer data or runs: permission and environment | Delivery lead; customer data/access approver | Approved sanitized inputs, documented use permission, permitted providers, isolated approved environment and least-privilege access demonstrated | Unapproved input, provider or access; environment isolation unproven |
| Before customer data or runs: evaluation and recovery | Evaluation lead; human reviewer; operations owner | Frozen baseline/held-out design and failure criteria; reviewer acceptance; incident contacts and response procedure; retention, deletion and export plan actually tested for the chosen deployment | Missing review authority or failed handling/recovery test |
| Before rollout or recurrence: performance and economics | Delivery lead; commercial lead | Actual negative controls, quality/failure evidence, repeated delivery hours, all-in cost and contribution against an agreed threshold | Quality gate missed, uncertainty too large, uncounted material cost or unsustainable repeat effort |
| Before rollout or recurrence: operation and exit | Operations owner; commercial lead; customer handover owner | Exercised billing, access grant/revocation, usable export and incident-response paths; accepted operating limits and deletion ownership | Billing/exit/recovery path untested or no owner able to operate it |

Agreement prompts are not contract language or legal advice. Obtain appropriate review for the actual engagement. Passing one gate never authorizes a broader environment or workflow.

## Conditional 30 / 60 / 90-day sequence

These are planning windows from qualification of a first pilot, not delivery promises. Move only when evidence clears the next gate; otherwise narrow the scope or stop.

- **Days 1–30:** assign accountable owners and backup, establish capacity, qualify one workflow, settle terms and complete pre-run gates. Freeze a small evaluation and deliver a reviewed report if the conditions permit.
- **Days 31–60:** address findings through an agreed scoped change. Repeat with fresh held-out samples, count actual review/rework hours, test handover and determine whether delivery contribution remains viable.
- **Days 61–90:** consider a recurring offer only after repeatable quality, capacity, economics and operational exit paths are evidenced. Agree cadence, price, responsibilities and support boundaries separately; do not infer them from a successful pilot.

Agent markets need demonstrated buyer demand, provider permission, quality evaluation, commercial terms, payment/dispute handling and sustainable unit economics before investment in a market. Robotics additionally needs a defined physical use case, competent safety review, hardware/integration partners, liability allocation and a controlled test setting. Both remain commercially gated research directions, with no autonomous operation or hardware launch commitment.
